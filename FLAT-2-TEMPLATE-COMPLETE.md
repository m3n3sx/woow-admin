## Design Philosophy

The Flat 2.0 template embodies the principles of modern flat design:

1. **No Skeuomorphism:** Completely flat with no attempt to mimic real-world materials
2. **Bold Colors:** Bright, saturated colors (#3498db, #2ecc71, #e74c3c)
3. **Geometric Shapes:** Sharp edges (0px radius) or slight rounding (4px)
4. **Bold Typography:** Heavy font weights (600-700) for strong visual hierarchy
5. **No Depth:** No shadows, gradients, or glassmorphism effects
6. **High Contrast:** Strong color contrasts for clarity
7. **Instant Feedback:** No animations (0s transition speed)
