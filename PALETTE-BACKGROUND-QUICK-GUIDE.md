# Quick Guide: Palette Background Colors

## What's New? 🎨

When you apply a color palette, the **Background Customization** tab now automatically updates with colors that match your chosen palette!

## How to Use

### Step 1: Choose a Palette
1. Go to **WOOW! Admin** settings
2. Click on the **Palettes** tab
3. Browse the available palettes

### Step 2: Apply the Palette
1. Click on your favorite palette
2. Click the **"Apply Palette"** button
3. Wait for the success message

### Step 3: Check the Background
1. Navigate to the **Background Customization** tab
2. You'll see the colors have automatically updated!
3. The gradient colors now match your palette

### Step 4: Fine-Tune (Optional)
- You can still manually adjust any background colors
- Your custom changes will be saved
- Applying a new palette will override your custom colors

## What Gets Updated?

When you apply a palette, these background settings update automatically:

✅ **Background Color** - The base color of your admin panel  
✅ **Gradient Start** - The starting color of the gradient  
✅ **Gradient End** - The ending color of the gradient  
✅ **Gradient Type** - Linear, radial, or conic  
✅ **Gradient Angle** - The direction of the gradient  

## Example: Professional Blue Palette

**Before applying:**
- Background: Random colors
- Gradient: Not coordinated

**After applying Professional Blue:**
- Background Color: `#dbeafe` (light blue)
- Gradient Start: `#dbeafe` (light blue)
- Gradient End: `#e0e7ff` (light indigo)
- Result: Beautiful blue gradient that matches the palette!

## Tips & Tricks

### Tip 1: Preview Before Applying
- Hover over a palette to see its colors
- Check if the background colors match your brand

### Tip 2: Experiment Freely
- Try different palettes to see which looks best
- You can always switch back
- Your previous settings are backed up automatically

### Tip 3: Customize After Applying
- Apply a palette as a starting point
- Then fine-tune the background colors to your liking
- This saves time compared to starting from scratch

### Tip 4: Match Your Brand
- Choose a palette with colors close to your brand
- Then adjust the background colors to match exactly
- This ensures consistency across your admin panel

## Troubleshooting

### Background colors didn't update?
1. Hard refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Check that you clicked "Apply Palette" (not just selected it)
3. Navigate to the Background Customization tab to see changes

### Colors don't match the palette preview?
1. Make sure you're looking at the Background Customization tab
2. Check that the palette was applied successfully
3. Try applying the palette again

### Want to reset to default?
1. Go to Background Customization tab
2. Click "Reset to Defaults" button
3. Or apply a different palette
